**It can't be done. No finite speed gets your average for the whole trip up to 60 km/h.**

- To average 60 km/h over 120 km, the whole trip has to take 120 ÷ 60 = **2 hours**.
- The first 60 km at 30 km/h already took 60 ÷ 30 = **2 hours**.

So you've already used the full time budget, and there are 0 hours left for the remaining 60 km. However fast you drive, the average stays below 60 km/h. It only gets close as your speed approaches infinity.

For reference, here is what different speeds give you on the second half:
- 90 km/h: 2 h 40 min total, 45 km/h average
- 120 km/h: 2.5 h total, 48 km/h average

The obvious answer, "90 km/h, because (30 + 90) / 2 = 60", is wrong. Average speed is total distance divided by total time, and you spend much longer at the slow speed than at the fast one.
